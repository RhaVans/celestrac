CELESTRAC

Every orbit. One picture.

Celestrac builds the sensors and the intelligence layer that track objects in space and activity on Earth, for operators who cannot afford to guess.

Request a briefing See how it works

01 / Intelligence

Intelligence that starts at the sensor.

Celestrac fuses orbital, aerial and ground data into a single operational picture. Every track carries its source, its confidence and its age, so the person making the call knows exactly what they are looking at.

Provenance
Every track traces back to its sensor.
Confidence
Scored on every track, never hidden.
Connectivity
Runs connected or fully disconnected.

Sense. Fuse. Decide. Deliver.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Our payloads and partner feeds collect the raw signal.

  2. 02

    Fuse

  3. 03

    Decide

  4. 04

    Deliver
