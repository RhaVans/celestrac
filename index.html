    Tycho

    The orbital sensor satellite: wide-field imaging from a three-axis bus.

    • Sun-synchronous orbit at 600 km, 98.0° inclination.
    • 0.42 m aperture, 1 120 kg launch mass.
    • On-board compression before the X-band downlink.

    Sextant

    The ground terminal: a transportable station that closes the link.

    • 2.4 m dish in two cases a two-person crew can carry.
    • X-band receive and S-band command on one mount.
    • Runs from vehicle power or a six-hour battery.
